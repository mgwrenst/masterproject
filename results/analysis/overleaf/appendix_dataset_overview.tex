\toprule
Operation & Description \\
\midrule
Column selection & Source-specific administrative, surrogate-key and unused metadata columns are removed from company, ownership and person-role files. \\
Column renaming & Raw source names are mapped to Norwegian, query-oriented names such as \texttt{orgNr}, \texttt{navn}, \texttt{selskapRolle}, \texttt{eierskapAndel} and \texttt{naceBeskrivelse}. \\
NACE enrichment & Company files are joined with \texttt{nace.csv} so that \texttt{naceKode} is accompanied by \texttt{naceBeskrivelse}. \\
Gender mapping & Known gender UUID values are converted to \texttt{Mann} and \texttt{Kvinne}. \\
Date normalization & Known date fields are converted to UTC ISO 8601 strings where parsing succeeds. \\
Name normalization & Shareholder names in the shareholder-register file are title-cased. \\
Company merge & Processed active-company and bankrupt-company temporary files are concatenated into the final \texttt{selskap.csv}. \\
